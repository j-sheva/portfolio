part 1 · geo audit

Pages search engines could index, but AI could not quote

I audited 5 representative brand pages out of 67. They had the data but no summary, no answers to common questions and no structured data, so AI assistants had little to understand or cite.

part 2 · landing pages

Pages that lead to a next step

Childcare costs: a calculator that leads somewhere

The calculator gave parents a number and stopped there. I reviewed the product requirements against the live page, wrote an FAQ for both search and AI assistants, and designed three options where the result leads to finding care.

Vacation care and family day care pages

Before redesigning, I looked at GA4 by device, source and behaviour, and compared how people searched for each type of care. The new pages were built around what the data showed.

part 3 · data before tests

Check who the traffic is before testing anything

Sales asked for A/B tests on listing cards, the enquiry flow and postcode search. Before planning them, I checked the analytics for the postcode pages and found that most of the visits looked non-human. A test on that traffic would have measured bots.

What I took from it

AI search changes what a good page is: clear answers, structure and trust signals, not only keywords.

Check who your traffic is before you test anything. Clean data saves weeks of misleading results.

Tool choices are product decisions too: start with the simplest tool that answers the question.
