context

Powerful data, unclear value

Qualoo maps and analyses network quality with data contributed by its users. For people without a technical background, both the value and the functionality were hard to understand.

No clear entry point for new usersYYuliia
Complex flows for contributing dataYYuliia
Little transparency on how it works and why it mattersYYuliia

Goal: make the product accessible to everyday users with clear flows, a better understanding of its value and more motivation to contribute.

key decisions

Three decisions that shaped the redesign

Decision 1

Show the value before asking for effort

For a complex product, a simpler interface is not enough. People need to see why their contribution matters, so onboarding leads with the mission and the reward.

Decision 2

Turn technical testing into one clear action

People did not need to understand network mapping to contribute. They needed one clear next step, so complex features became simple, step-by-step interactions.

Decision 3

Make contribution a habit

Daily tasks, rewards and leaderboards give people a reason to come back and contribute consistently.

components

Reusable pieces for both platforms

What I took from it

For complex products, simplifying the interface is half the job. The other half is communicating the value.

Breaking technical processes into simple actions improves both understanding and engagement.
