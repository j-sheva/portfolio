context

Why carbon trading felt hard

Aetlas is a B2B platform where companies buy and sell carbon credits. The domain mixes finance, sustainability and verification, and businesses need trust, clarity and transparent data before they commit.

An unfamiliar domain for many companiesYYuliia
Low trust and transparency in transactionsYYuliia
Projects were hard to evaluate and compareYYuliia
Overwhelming flows for buyers and sellersYYuliia

Goal: a clear and trustworthy B2B experience that simplifies carbon trading, improves transparency and supports confident decisions.

key decisions

From raw data to a confident decision

Decision 1

Make projects comparable in one table

Complex financial and environmental data had to become structured and digestible, so businesses can evaluate projects side by side with confidence.

Decision 2

Keep the trust signals next to the commitment

Carbon trading needs trust and transparent data before a company commits, so verification and project details stay in view while the buyer decides.

Decision 3

Design the seller side as carefully as the buyer side

A marketplace needs both sides to work. Project developers get their own portal for commitments, offers and digital offtakes.

design system

One system for two portals

What I took from it

B2B products need a strong focus on clarity, structure and support for decision-making.

Transparent data and lower cognitive load help businesses navigate a complex domain and trust the product.
